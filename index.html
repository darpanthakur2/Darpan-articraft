Tap Step as fast as you can to build speed (keys: Left, Right, A, D or Space). Sprint taps give big bursts but use stamina. Holding a button only jogs slowly. A better outfit adds a small speed bonus, but your tapping decides how fast you are.

Ten festival tasks

Each task has a target time. Finish at half the target or faster for double coins; the reward shrinks the longer you take, down to a quarter. Blessings from the Mandir add 10% each. Tasks are optional side quests that pay coins for outfits with a small speed boost, but the leaderboard clock keeps running while you do them. The street is 4 km and the run ends at the Mela gate.

Shubh Deepawali!

Crackers

Fifteen crackers, and each one costs more than the one before. Keys 1 to 9 burst the first nine. Every blast says Happy Diwali to all.

Mandir

Offer a flower, light the diya and ring the bell at each shrine to receive a blessing: +10% coins on every task.

Bazaar

Spend the coins you earn from tasks.

My lamps

Lamps you buy in the Bazaar wait here. Light them to make them glow on the rooftops along the street, and put them out again whenever you like.

Wardrobe

Every outfit you have bought is kept here. Wear any of them, or take it off to go back to plain clothes. Only the outfit you wear gives its speed bonus.

Festival rooms

Pick a room to join.

Leaderboard

Fastest runs to the Mela gate at 4 km. The clock starts on your first step, keeps running while you do tasks or shop, and stops at the gate.
